# Code of Conduct

Plunger is a small project run in the open. We want it to be a pleasant place to report a bug, ask a question, or send a fix, whoever you are and however much experience you have.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most confusion is a misunderstanding, not an attack.
- **Be specific and constructive.** Criticise code, ideas and behaviour, not people.
- **Be patient**, especially with newcomers and with maintainers, who are mostly volunteers.
- **Respect decisions.** Plunger is deliberately small, and "this doesn't fit" is not a judgment on you.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or spam.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow this. To report a problem, use a private channel: open a [private security advisory](SECURITY.md) marked "conduct", or message a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
